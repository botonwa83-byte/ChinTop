## English

ChinTop is an offline Chinese-language learning app. All questions and explanations are bundled in the app; there is no sign-up, no login and no server communication.

- Features: practice and explanations for five modules (modern reading, classical Chinese, poetry, writing, integrated skills), plus ability map, mistake review, growth report and method cards.
- Free tier: the first 3 questions of every module for each grade level; ability map, mistake review, growth report and method cards are free forever.
- Paid tier: one-time non-consumable unlock (product ID `com.chintop.app.full_unlock`, CNY 22) that opens the complete question bank.
- IAP test path: open the app → "功能" tab → "解锁完整版" to see the paywall. Use a sandbox account to purchase, or tap "恢复购买" with an already-purchased account.
- No third-party SDKs, no ads, no analytics, no data uploaded.
